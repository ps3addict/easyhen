# Grab both files and place them in root of USB drive.

Download [PS3HEN.BIN](PS3HEN.BIN)

Download [HEN_UPD.pkg](HEN_UPD.pkg)

Plug the USB drive in the slot closest to disc drive on your PS3.

Open my website (ps3addict.github.io) in your PS3 browser.

Click on "HEN Enablers" link.

Click on the second link on that page (for USB000.html)

HEN should enable and install. Reboot when done!

** Do not leave pkg file on USB with it plugged into PS3 after installation!
** It will be found by HEN and reinstalled everytime you enable HEN!
